Fixed difficulty check
A session fails this script if its difficulty changes.

easy    sessions=5  changes=0  mean_survival=8.00s  mean_health=96.0  mean_reward=0.484
medium  sessions=5  changes=0  mean_survival=8.00s  mean_health=76.0  mean_reward=0.609
hard    sessions=5  changes=0  mean_survival=6.44s  mean_health=0.0  mean_reward=0.016

Fixed difficulty stayed on the selected level in every session.

[exit code 0]
